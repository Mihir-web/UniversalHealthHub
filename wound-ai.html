Inside the engine

State-of-the-art medical computer vision

A powerful AI engine combining MedSAM-2 segmentation with the latest DINOv3 vision backbone — best-in-class performance in medical computer vision.

Segmentation

MedSAM-2

Medical-grade segmentation that isolates wound boundaries with sub-pixel precision — the foundation for every downstream measurement, including area, depth proxy, and tissue type classification.

Vision Backbone

DINOv3

The latest self-supervised vision backbone, fine-tuned on 8,000+ clinical wound images for stage and tissue classification — outperforming general-purpose vision models in real-world wound scenarios.
